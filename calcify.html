About Us

Calcify is designed to be your ultimate all-in-one calculation hub. We believe that everyday math shouldn't require jumping between ten different messy apps or ad-filled websites.

Our mission is to provide powerful, lightning-fast, and beautiful calculators wrapped in a clean Bento-grid interface that respects your time and your data.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Calcify free to use? +
Yes, Calcify is 100% free with no hidden fees or subscriptions.
Do you store my calculation data? +
No. All math is performed locally in your browser. We don't see or save any of your numbers.
Can I use this offline? +
Yes! Once the page is loaded, it runs entirely on Javascript, so you can disconnect from the internet and keep calculating.
